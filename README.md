# Paywalled Sites

A community-maintained list of websites that contain paywalls or registration walls (regwalls).

This list is used by our [search engine](https://moosearch.com/) to provide transparency information alongside search results, helping users identify websites that require a subscription, payment, or registration to view their content.

We have decided to make this list publicly available so anyone can use it.

## Sources

The list is compiled from the following sources:

- [bpc-fetch](https://github.com/Sophomoresty/bpc-fetch)
- Manual additions and community contributions.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions! If you know of a website that uses a paywall or registration wall and isn't already listed, feel free to open a pull request.

We will review submitted domains to ensure they are accurate and meet the list's criteria.

Please make sure that:
- The domain actually has a paywall or registration wall.
- The domain isn't already present in the list.
- The submitted entry is correctly formatted.
- Domains that do not have paywalls on all their articles belong on possibly_paywalled.txt
